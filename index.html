Controls & Build Guide

Step-by-step help for placing objects, changing chemistry, and building a living planet.

1. Spawn and navigate

  1. Use the left sidebar tabs to choose a category, then select an object or tool.
  2. Click or tap the main universe to place the selected object. Use ＋ Spawn selected if you prefer a button; drag across the canvas to paint a trail.
  3. Press ☰ to collapse or reopen the sidebar and make more room.

2. Change atoms and molecules

  1. Select Matter → Configure Planet / Star in the sidebar, then click or tap the existing planet or star. This opens its editor without spawning anything. Double-click or double-tap also works.
  2. In the chemistry editor, change the number fields under Atoms and Molecules. Molecule cards include diagrams to show their structures.
  3. Press Save composition to apply the changes. The new composition changes the object's mass and size; on planets it also affects atmosphere, water, and greenhouse behavior, while a star's hydrogen changes its light output.

Tip: Configure Planet / Star selects existing objects without placing new ones. The editor opens for regular Planets and Stars; surface and minerals controls appear only for a regular Planet.

3. Build a planet's surface

  1. Double-click a regular Planet to open Configure planet.
  2. Choose Green Planet or Dead Planet to fill in a starting composition, or edit the values yourself.
  3. Set Vegetation and adjust the amount of each rock or mineral. These values change the planet's tint and contribute to its mass.
  4. Press Save composition. The updated surface appears in the main universe.

4. Prepare a world for life

  1. In the planet editor, add water molecules (H₂O) or surface Ice. The life check requires water and ice to make up at least 35% of the surface composition.
  2. Save, then select Matter → Planet Cell Lab in the sidebar and click the regular Planet.
  3. In the microscope view, choose a cell, plant, or animal from the right panel and click the surface to place it. Plants need light; grazers move toward and eat plants. Use Organism Builder to select 2–12 placed cells in order, name them, and assemble a custom multicellular creature; swimmer cells help it move, grazer cells seek and eat plants, and photosynthetic cells gather energy.

A planet that is too rocky cannot open the Cell Lab yet. Add more H₂O or Ice in Configure planet, save, and try again.

5. Other useful controls

  • Inspect: select Inspect, then click an object to see its properties and behavior.
  • Grab / Drag: select it, then drag an object to move it.
  • Fusion: select Fusion or Tri-Fusion and choose the required objects; use the Combination Codex in the menu for recipes and ordering hints.
  • Planet orbit: choose Orbit Tool, click a planet, then click a star.
  • Top controls: toggle magnetic field lines and orbits, pause the simulation, change speed, reset, or switch fullscreen.
  • Keyboard: Space pauses or resumes, R resets, and Esc closes an open menu or guide.
